Privacy Policy

For Shetkari AI · Provided by KrishnaGrid Technologies · Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shetkari AI, developed and maintained by KrishnaGrid Technologies, may handle information when you use the app. The app’s actual data practices depend on the features and service providers in use. Please ensure this policy accurately reflects the live app before publishing.

Information and crop images

Crop analysis may request access to your camera or photos so you can submit an image for analysis. Camera or photo access is used only after you grant permission. Images and information you provide may be processed to generate a response. Avoid including people or documents in crop photos.

AI and third-party services

Some features may use third-party AI or infrastructure providers to process submitted questions or images. Information necessary to provide the requested feature may be shared with those providers and handled under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Data security and retention

KrishnaGrid Technologies takes reasonable steps to protect information processed by Shetkari AI. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Information should be retained only as long as needed to provide the service or meet applicable obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to grant camera or photo permissions and change those permissions in device settings. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Contact contact@krishnagrid.com.

Children and policy updates

Shetkari AI is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate consent. We may update this policy as the app or applicable requirements change.

Before publishing: Confirm this policy matches the app’s real permissions, AI providers, analytics, storage, and retention practices.
